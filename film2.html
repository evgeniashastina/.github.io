Homesick (Work in progress)

Homesick is an animated original story, following the main character - Vika, a teenage girl, who runs away from her dysfunctional family. In a new place, she feels ready to start a new life, but the more time passes, the more this life reminds her of her old one, which she wanted to escape from so much. The new friends just disappear and Vika is left alone again. She doesn't know what to do and just keeps running away from the town and from herself.

Animation: Evgenia Shastina

Music: Kimi Korpi
